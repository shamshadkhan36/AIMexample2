OUR PROCESS

5-Stage Investigation Lifecycle

Every assignment follows rigorous operational checks to maintain evidentiary purity and legal compliance.

Stage 01
Case Discussion

Secure intake of claim files, scope alignment, and turnaround agreement.

Stage 02
Case Assessment
Initial document cross-check, anomaly detection, and field dispatch planning.
Stage 03
Investigation & Verification
Physical field visits, hospital register audits, and geo-tagged documentation.
Stage 04
Analysis & Quality Audit
Internal peer review, evidentiary cross-matching, and timeline validation.
Stage 05
Investigation Report
Submission of encrypted final dossier with photographic and factual evidence.
